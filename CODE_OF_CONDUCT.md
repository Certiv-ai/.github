# Code of conduct

Certiv wants its public project spaces to be useful, respectful, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and specific.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Protect private, customer, and security-sensitive information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope and moderation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption,
doxxing, and publication of private or security-sensitive information are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, limit
participation, or ban accounts when necessary to protect the community and
project.

Until a dedicated conduct-reporting route is approved, use the
[Certiv company contact form](https://certiv.ai/company/#contact), mark the
message **Code of conduct**, and do not include sensitive evidence in the first
message.
