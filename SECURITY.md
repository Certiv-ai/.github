# Security policy

Do not report vulnerabilities, exposed credentials, or sensitive security
findings in a public issue.

Until Certiv publishes an approved dedicated security-reporting route, use the
[Certiv company contact form](https://certiv.ai/company/#contact) and clearly
mark the message **Security report**. Include:

- the affected repository and version or commit;
- reproduction steps;
- potential impact;
- a safe way to coordinate follow-up.

Do not include secrets, customer data, or exploit material in the initial
message.

Individual repositories may define a more specific security policy. When they
do, that repository's policy takes precedence.
